## Outcome

Rejected the bounded layer candidate because its texture resampling visibly
softens revealed text at panel scale 0.78. It fixes caret coverage, but does not
meet the unchanged-sharpness requirement. Production QML is byte-for-byte the
baseline; no provider, authentication, API, dependency or configuration changes.
No repair kit or human repeat is warranted. Broader mitigations require a later
plan. This is a completed diagnosis/rejection handoff, not G07 acceptance.

The [upstream draft](UQC-218-UPSTREAM.md) contains an independent QtQuick
reproduction, pinned Qt 6.11.2 source trace, measured results, a rasterization
interpretation explicitly labelled as inference, and sanitized PNG attachments.
It has not been submitted upstream.

## Implementation and evidence

- `tests/diagnostics/qtquick-caret/`: standalone CMake executable and disposable
  software/private-Sway runner; no HoloNight modules, Controls or platform theme.
  Geometry errors exit 2, visibility failures exit 1. Both backends reproduce
  zero pixels at DPR 1 and 23 at DPR 1.25 at the recorded geometry.
- `tests/runtime/controls_test.cpp`: opt-in `DISABLED_PasswordCaretLayerCandidate`
  applies the layer exclusively inside the fixture, preserves exact geometry
  assertions, and exercises empty/populated/cleared, focus re-entry, masking,
  navigation, long-text scrolling and direct/layer text comparisons. The
  original unmodified Qt diagnostic and all visibility assertions remain intact.
- `scripts/check-caret-graphics.py --layer-candidate`: explicit, mutually exclusive
  diagnostic mode, separate from the original `--exact-geometry` and ordinary
  normal/compact coverage. A passing candidate test is not visual acceptance.

All eight candidate style/backend/DPR cases pass the visibility checks. Empty,
cleared and refocused fields change 36 pixels at DPR 1 and 23 at DPR 1.25 in both
styles/backends. Each case also passes 15 subpixel samples: diagonal displacement
0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1 physical pixel, at overall scales 0.78, 1 and 1.25.
The sampled field retains 420×57 logical dimensions; mapped cursor widths are
asserted for each scale. Full-window on/off/difference captures contain only
synthetic input. Direct/layer comparison holds geometry, text and style constant.
The paired text crops show the resampling softening that rejects this candidate.
This is a visual assessment, not a new automated sharpness threshold.

Native blink timing, exhaustive independent x/y sweeps, layer resize/refresh
acceptance and a human sharpness decision were not completed after the visual
rejection. They are not claimed as passes. A later successful candidate must
complete those checks, full launch/install checks, ordinary-CI production
regression, immutable repair-kit release, and four human comparisons. Enabling
an unfixed production regression in ordinary CI would misrepresent this result;
the external failure remains explicit opt-in.
